Plain-language summary

We collect only what is reasonably needed to operate, support, secure, and bill for B.O.T.D. The current coaching application may store much of your playbook data locally in your browser. Stripe handles card details. We do not sell personal information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how FENRIR LLC (“FENRIR,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use botdhockey.com, B.O.T.D. Hockey Coaching Aid, customer support, checkout, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

This Policy does not govern third-party services that publish their own privacy notices, including payment processors, identity providers, browser vendors, and linked websites.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact and account information: name, email address, organization or team information, login information when account features are available, and support communications.
  • Coaching content: play titles, team names and colors, rosters, player names or nicknames, jersey numbers, positions, handedness, routes, puck events, timing, coaching notes, drawings, and uploaded artwork.
  • Purchase information: product, price, promotion, tax, subscription status, transaction identifiers, billing contact information, and receipt history. FENRIR does not directly receive or store full payment-card numbers or security codes.
  • Communications: questions, feedback, bug reports, and other messages you send to us.

Information collected automatically

Our website, hosting providers, security providers, and payment providers may collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, timestamps, referral information, errors, and security events. We use this information for delivery, troubleshooting, fraud prevention, security, and service improvement.

3. Local browser and device storage

The current B.O.T.D. application may store plays, rosters, preferences, drawings, uploaded logos, and related content in browser storage on the device you use. This data may not be transmitted to FENRIR unless you deliberately export, share, submit, or use a hosted account or cloud feature.

Local browser data can be lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, reset the browser, remove the application, change devices, or lose access to the device. Use available export or backup tools for important work.

We may use cookies or similar storage that is necessary to remember settings, maintain sessions, protect the Service, or support checkout. Payment providers may use their own cookies and fraud-prevention technologies under their privacy notices.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • Create and manage accounts, subscriptions, trials, and access entitlements.
  • Process purchases, renewals, taxes, cancellations, refunds, and receipts.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service or policy updates.
  • Protect users, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot errors, and enforce our Terms.
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.
  • Analyze product performance using aggregated or de-identified information where practical.

We do not use coach-entered youth-player information for advertising profiles.

5. How we disclose information

We may disclose information:

  • To service providers that host, secure, support, analyze, communicate, or process payments for the Service, subject to appropriate contractual or legal duties.
  • At your direction when you share, export, publish, or send a play or invite another person to access content.
  • For legal and safety reasons when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or respond to lawful process.
  • In a business transaction involving financing, reorganization, sale, merger, or transfer of FENRIR assets, subject to applicable law.

FENRIR does not sell personal information for money and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Payments and Stripe

Stripe processes B.O.T.D. subscription payments. When you check out, Stripe may collect your name, email, billing address, payment credentials, device information, and fraud signals. Stripe provides FENRIR with transaction and subscription information but not your full card number or card security code.

Stripe’s handling of information is governed by its own terms and privacy notice. You should review those materials on the Stripe checkout page.

7. Youth-player information

B.O.T.D. is designed for adult coaches, instructors, administrators, and other authorized users. It is not directed to children under 13, and children should not purchase subscriptions or create adult contracting accounts.

Coaches may choose to enter limited information about youth players. The coach or organization is responsible for deciding whether it has authority to do so and for complying with team, school, league, parental-consent, and privacy obligations.

Use the minimum information needed. We recommend initials, nicknames, jersey numbers, or position-only records when a full legal name is unnecessary. Do not enter player addresses, personal contact details, birth dates, medical information, government identifiers, or other sensitive data into coaching rosters or notes.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information directly to FENRIR without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security and business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with tax, accounting, and legal requirements. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose.

Locally stored application data remains under the control of your browser or device. Transaction and tax records may need to be retained after an account or subscription ends. We may retain aggregated or de-identified information that no longer reasonably identifies an individual.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the Service. No internet transmission, browser storage, or information system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee that unauthorized access or loss will never occur.

You can help by securing your device and email account, using unique credentials, limiting the personal information entered into rosters, and maintaining backups of important locally stored plays.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on where you live and subject to legal exceptions, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information FENRIR controls. You may also object to or restrict certain processing and withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.

To make a request, email fenrir@botdhockey.com from the address associated with your purchase or account. We may need to verify your identity. We will not ask you to send a password, full card number, or card security code by email.

You may control local application data through B.O.T.D.’s delete/export tools, when available, or through your browser’s site-data settings. Canceling a subscription is addressed in our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

11. International use

FENRIR is based in Washington State, United States. If you access the Service from another country, information processed by FENRIR or its providers may be transferred to and handled in the United States or other jurisdictions where providers operate. Those jurisdictions may have different data-protection laws.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service, providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required.
