How it works

From coaching idea to shared playbook.

Build the sequence, revise the details, and keep it ready whenever the team needs to see it.

STEP 01

Set the ice

Load the players, goalies, and staff you need from the two team benches. Hide the benches when the setup is complete.

STEP 02

Build the play or drill

Draw each player’s movement, then add passes, shots, possession, timing, and visual coaching cues.

STEP 03

Save, share, and teach

Replay the sequence, revise any moment, save it to your playbook, then export or share it with players and staff.

Questions

Before you step onto the ice.

What devices does B.O.T.D. support?
B.O.T.D. runs in a modern web browser and is designed for desktop and iPad workflows, including touch and Apple Pencil-style input. Browser and device behavior can vary, so review important plays before a practice or presentation.
Can I change one player without rebuilding the entire play?
Yes. Pause at the frame where the decision changes, select that player, and trace a new route. The route before the playhead remains intact, while only that player’s future is replaced.
Can I use B.O.T.D. for drills as well as plays?
Yes. Use the same rink, routes, puck actions, timing, and visual cues to build set plays, systems, skills stations, small-area games, warmups, and other coaching sequences.
Can I keep and share a playbook?
Yes. Save and organize plays, drills, and variations with the local playbook tools, then export or share finished sequences with players and staff. Keep exported backups of important material, especially before clearing browser data or changing devices.
Do the monthly and annual plans include the same features?
Yes. Both options include the complete Coach Pro feature set. Monthly billing lowers the upfront commitment; annual billing provides the best value and saves $40.88 compared with 12 monthly payments.
Can I cancel or request a refund?
You may cancel future renewal at any time. Initial purchases may be eligible for a refund when requested within 14 calendar days. Review the Refund and Cancellation Policy for details.
Is the off-side indicator an official ruling tool?
No. It is an instructional visualization aid based on simplified player and puck representations. Actual rulings depend on the applicable rulebook, skate position, possession, timing, and official judgment.
