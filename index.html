Product mission

Make every hockey idea easier to see.

Static arrows show where a play might go. B.O.T.D. shows when it happens, how it changes, and what every player is doing at the same moment.

B.O.T.D. exists to make hockey instruction easier to see, easier to revise, and easier to share—so coaches spend less time explaining static diagrams and more time teaching decisions, timing, and movement.

Built around the play

A coaching board that understands time.

Every control is designed to help a coach move from an idea to a playable sequence without burying the rink under software.

01

One route per player

Trace movement for each skater independently, then watch the full unit execute together on a synchronized timeline.

02

Change any moment

Pause the play, select one player, and redraw only what happens from that frame forward—without teleporting or resetting everyone else.

03

How it works

From clean ice to complete play.

Build the sequence in three direct steps, then refine it until the timing communicates exactly what you want.

STEP 01

Set the ice

Load the players, goalies, and staff you need from the two team benches. Hide the benches when the setup is complete.

STEP 02

Trace the decisions

Draw each player’s movement, then add passes, shots, possession, and visual coaching cues.

STEP 03

Play, pause, revise

Watch the sequence, stop at the exact frame that needs work, and redraw only the future you want to change.

Questions

Before you step onto the ice.

What devices does B.O.T.D. support?
B.O.T.D. runs in a modern web browser and is designed for desktop and iPad workflows, including touch and Apple Pencil-style input. Browser and device behavior can vary, so review important plays before a practice or presentation.
Can I change one player without rebuilding the entire play?
Yes. Pause at the frame where the decision changes, select that player, and trace a new route. The route before the playhead remains intact, while only that player’s future is replaced.
How are promotion codes handled?

Can I cancel or request a refund?
You may cancel future renewal at any time. Initial purchases may be eligible for a refund when requested within 14 calendar days. Review the Refund and Cancellation Policy for details.
Is the off-side indicator an official ruling tool?
No. It is an instructional visualization aid based on simplified player and puck representations. Actual rulings depend on the applicable rulebook, skate position, possession, timing, and official judgment.
