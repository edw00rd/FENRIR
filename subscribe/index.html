Checkout questions

Do both options include the same features?
Yes. Monthly and annual billing both unlock the complete Coach Pro feature set. Only the price and renewal interval differ.
What will I be charged?
The annual option is $79 USD per year. The monthly option is $9.99 USD per month. Applicable tax may be added. Stripe shows the amount due today and the recurring renewal amount before you subscribe.
Does the subscription renew?
Yes. The option you choose renews automatically at its stated monthly or annual interval until canceled. Your Stripe checkout page and receipt confirm the applicable renewal terms.
How do I get access?
Account-access and setup instructions are sent to the email address used during checkout. For help, contact support@botdhockey.com.
Can I cancel?
You may cancel future renewal at any time. Access ordinarily continues through the paid period. Read the Refund and Cancellation Policy for details.
