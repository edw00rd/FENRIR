Cancel whenever you choose. Cancellation stops the next renewal, and access ordinarily continues through the paid period. Initial purchases may be refunded when requested within 14 days. Promotional first-year prices may renew at the standard annual price shown at checkout.

1. Policy summary

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to paid subscriptions for B.O.T.D. Hockey Coaching Aid purchased directly from FENRIR LLC. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

The checkout page and receipt identify the product, amount due, renewal price, promotion, tax, and billing interval that apply to a specific purchase. When those transaction-specific terms differ from a general example on this page, the terms clearly disclosed at checkout control, subject to applicable law.

2. Canceling a subscription

You may cancel future renewal at any time through any customer billing portal made available to you or by emailing fenrir@botdhockey.com.

Unless the checkout terms or applicable law provide otherwise:

  • Cancellation prevents the next automatic renewal.
  • Your paid access continues through the end of the current subscription period.
  • Cancellation does not automatically create a prorated refund for unused time.
  • Deleting a local copy of the application or ceasing use does not by itself cancel billing.

Submit cancellation requests before the renewal date. We recommend allowing enough time for us to identify and process an emailed request.

3. Initial-purchase refund window

You may request a refund of an initial B.O.T.D. subscription purchase within 14 calendar days after the charge. After that period, payments are non-refundable except where required by law or when FENRIR LLC is unable to provide the purchased Service.

The 14-day policy applies to the initial purchase, including a discounted first-year purchase, unless a different refund right is required by law. Abuse of promotion codes, fraud, chargeback manipulation, or material violation of the Terms may affect discretionary refund eligibility but will not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

4. Automatic renewals and renewal charges

B.O.T.D. Coach Pro is an automatically renewing annual subscription unless the checkout page states otherwise. The payment method on file may be charged at each renewal until cancellation.

Renewal charges are generally non-refundable once processed, except where required by law or where FENRIR approves an exception. If you believe a renewal was processed after a timely cancellation request, contact us promptly and include the cancellation evidence.

5. Promotions and Founding Coach pricing

Promotion codes may reduce the initial subscription charge without changing later renewal pricing. For example, a Founding Coach offer may reduce the first year from the standard $79 USD annual price to $49 USD, while later annual renewals remain $79 USD plus applicable tax, unless canceled.

The exact initial and renewal amounts displayed at checkout control. Promotion codes may have expiration dates, redemption limits, first-time-customer restrictions, product restrictions, and other conditions. Discounts have no cash value, cannot normally be combined, and may not be applied retroactively after checkout.

6. Taxes

Applicable sales or similar taxes may be added to the subscription charge. When a payment is refunded, associated tax will be handled as required by applicable law and the payment processor’s transaction records.

7. How to request a cancellation or refund

Email fenrir@botdhockey.com and include:

  • The email address used at checkout.
  • Your full name or organization name.
  • The date and amount of the charge.
  • Whether you are requesting cancellation, a refund, or both.
  • A brief explanation if the request concerns an error or inability to access the Service.

Do not email your password, complete card number, bank account number, or card security code. We may request additional non-sensitive information to locate and verify the transaction.

8. Refund processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through the payment processor. FENRIR can initiate the refund, but the time for it to appear depends on the payment network, financial institution, and account status.

Canceling a subscription and refunding a charge are separate actions. A refund does not always cancel future renewal, and cancellation does not automatically refund a completed charge. We will confirm the status of each action when processing a request.

9. Required legal rights

Nothing in this Policy limits a non-waivable consumer right, statutory cooling-off period, or remedy that applies to you. If local law grants a stronger right, FENRIR will honor that requirement.

If you have a billing concern, please contact us before initiating a payment dispute so we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve it. This request does not waive your lawful dispute rights or deadlines.
